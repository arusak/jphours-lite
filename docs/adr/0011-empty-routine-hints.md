# Derive empty Routine hints from saved entry content

The blank Routine factory and hint eligibility share one Exercise template: Practice, 100 BPM, and 300 seconds. Eligibility compares all current Exercise content fields while ignoring generated identities, Routine name, settings, and timestamps. This keeps imported or reopened matching Routines equivalent to newly created ones without onboarding metadata or storage changes.

Hints follow committed Routine entries, so saving different content hides them and restoring the template shows them again; opening or cancelling a draft changes nothing. The overlay is passive, with readable labels and decorative arrows anchored to explicit control refs. Individual annotations disappear while their targets are hidden or offscreen, without changing content eligibility or dismissing the other hints.
